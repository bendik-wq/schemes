Privacy policy

How we handle your data.

Last updated October 2026. Draft for review by a lawyer before launch.

Who we are

Asymmetrical AI Solutions is run by Asym, [postal address]. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: [contact email].

What we collect

  • What you give us: your name, email, mobile number and the answers in your application, and anything you add when you book a call.
  • How you use the site: pages viewed, how far you scroll, which sections you spend time on, how much of a video you watch, clicks, and timing on form fields. We never record what you type into a field before you submit it. If you select or copy text on our pages (not in forms), we keep up to 120 characters of it to understand which parts were useful; we never quote it back to you.
  • Technical data: IP address (or a hashed version of it), approximate location from your IP, browser and device type, and the link or ad that brought you here.
  • Emails and calls: whether our emails are opened and links clicked, and, for calls with our AI assistant, a recording, a transcript and a summary.

Cookies

We set our own cookies to recognise your browser: a visitor id (for up to 400 days, or only until you close the browser if you are in the EU, EEA, UK, Norway or Switzerland and have not accepted cookies), a session id (a new session starts after 30 minutes of inactivity; the cookie itself expires after 2 hours), your consent choice (180 days), which version of a page or industry page you saw (90 days), and, if you arrived through a client's personal link or saw a test version of a page, which link and which version (same rule as the visitor id: 90 days, or only until you close the browser). Someone who opens a client's personal link sees that client's first name, nothing else. With your consent we also set Meta advertising cookies (_fbp, _fbc). In the EU, EEA, UK, Norway and Switzerland, no advertising cookies are set, no ad pixel loads and nothing is shared with ad platforms until you accept. Session recording (with every form field masked) only runs if you accept. Fonts are served from our own server. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

How we use it

  • To review your application and decide whether a call makes sense. Applications are scored automatically on the answers you give; you can ask a person to look at any decision again.
  • To send you emails about your application, your call and the program. Some emails are written with the help of an AI model (Anthropic's Claude), using your application and how you used the site to make them relevant. We never quote back what you selected or copied on the page.
  • To call you with an AI assistant, only if you ticked the box that allows it. The assistant says it is an AI, says the call is recorded, and stops if you say stop. Calls only happen during local daytime hours for your phone number's country.
  • To measure and improve our ads and pages. With consent where it is required, we send Meta your email, phone, name and country in hashed (scrambled) form, plus your IP address, browser type and Meta cookie ids, so it can tell which ads lead to applications. Google Analytics receives a random visitor id and events, never your contact details or your application id.
  • If you are a client and send us a few words about your build, we only publish them after checking the wording with you, and only in the ways you ticked (on this site, with your name, with your company, in emails, in ads). Reply to any of our emails to withdraw it and we take it down.

Legal basis

Taking steps at your request before a contract (your application and call); our legitimate interest in following up and improving the funnel; and your consent for advertising cookies, ad-platform sharing and AI calls. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Who we share it with

Processors that run parts of the service for us, under contract:

  • Cloudflare (USA, global network): hosting, database, security.
  • Resend (USA): sending email.
  • Calendly (USA): booking calls.
  • Anthropic (USA): writing personalised emails.
  • ElevenLabs (USA/EU) and Twilio (USA): AI phone calls and recordings.
  • Vapi (USA): optional web and phone assistant.
  • PostHog (EU or USA, depending on our settings): product analytics and, with consent, session replay with inputs masked.
  • Meta (Ireland/USA) and Google (Ireland/USA): ad measurement, with consent where required.
  • Slack or Discord (USA): internal team alerts with your name, reference, application tier and call status.
  • Meta WhatsApp (Ireland/USA): if you message us on WhatsApp.
  • Our CRM, if connected: a copy of your contact details and application status for our sales team.

Transfers outside the EEA/UK rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Raw usage events are deleted after 13 months. After 13 months we also remove IP addresses, browser details, location, referrers, page addresses and ad click ids from visit records, and delete call recordings, call transcripts and WhatsApp message text. Application records of people we did not work with are deleted 6 years after the last activity. Clients' records are kept as long as accounting law requires. We erase everything we hold about you on request, and ask the processors that hold copies (for example our CRM, Calendly, ElevenLabs and Resend) to delete theirs. Payment amounts we must keep for accounting are kept without your name.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction, or to object to processing, and you can unsubscribe from emails with the link in every email (it stops every email at once). Email us at the address above. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority, for example Datatilsynet in Norway, the ICO in the UK, the OAIC in Australia or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner in Canada. We do not sell your personal information. If you live in a US state with privacy rights, you can use the same contact to exercise them.
