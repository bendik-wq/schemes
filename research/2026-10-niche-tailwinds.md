# Niche tailwinds that are fairly easy to launch (found bottom-up, not from the brief)

Research date: 25 October 2026. Prepared for Bendik / Asym.

**Brief:** find niches the data surfaces on its own: real tailwind, thin supply, launchable in about 60 days with low capex, and a good fit for an agency strong at funnels, CRM, ads and AI (Claude Code). Peptides, financing and everything in `2026-10-second-mover-offers.md` were excluded on purpose.

## Method

Five independent discovery tracks, then one shared validation pass:

1. **Bottom-up search data.** Rising Google searches for 40 broad seeds such as "certification", "tax credit", "inspection", "pet", "senior" and "contractor" (`data/google-trends-rising-broad-seeds-*.json`).
2. **Regulation, deadlines and policy.** New laws, tax changes, billing codes and government programmes that force or pay businesses to act. Each rule's 2026 status was checked: many were delayed or vacated.
3. **Second-order effects of big shifts.** AI and data-centre build-out, the silver tsunami, the home-insurance crisis, tariffs, school choice.
4. **Smart money into boring niches.** Private-equity roll-ups, franchise unit growth and BizBuySell multiples.
5. **Consumer demand.** Google Trends (seasonally matched), Yelp Trend Tracker, YouTube, Reddit growth and Eventbrite data.
6. **Validation.** 52 candidates scored on 5-year US Google Trends (year-on-year and 2-year growth) and on YouTube attention against creator supply (`data/validate-*.json`).

**Signal of note:** the new **federal $1,700 scholarship tax credit** was found independently by three of the five tracks.

## The ranked shortlist

Score (out of 10) = verified tailwind × thin supply × ease of launch × fit with Asym.

### 1. Claude Certification Academy + "Claude Partner" practice setup — 9/10 · run it yourself

**Why**
- Anthropic launched its first official certification (Claude Certified Architect – Foundations) on 12 March 2026. By July it had four exams.
- Exams are proctored through Pearson VUE ($125; Professional $175) and valid for 12 months, which means **recurring recertification demand**.
- Several guides say candidates must work at a Claude Partner Network organisation. **Verify this in Anthropic's official FAQ.**
- Google searches for "Claude certification" are up 14× year on year. "Claude certified architect" was the fastest-rising "certified" query in our seed scan (+7,550%).
- On YouTube, prep videos drew 897k views in 12 months (top video 381k). Prep providers are scattered blogs.
- Sources:
  - https://yu-wenhao.com/en/blog/anthropic-claude-certifications/
  - https://www.claudedirectory.org/blog/anthropic-claude-certification-program
  - https://datrick.com/claude-certifications

**Offer**
- **(a) Online prep:** practice exams, labs, a cohort bootcamp. $297–997 per person.
- **(b) Team certification sprints for companies:** $5–15k per team.
- **(c) "Claude Partner Launch" for agencies and consultancies:**
  - Partner-network application and partner positioning.
  - Certifying their team.
  - A productised Claude Code service menu.
  - Lead engine.
  - Price: $15–25k.

**Why you:** you already sell Claude Code builds. This is your authority product and a feeder for the $1M+ founder offer.

**Ease:** content plus funnel in 2–4 weeks; no licence needed.

**Risks**
- Anthropic's rules for third-party prep and partners. Never imply affiliation; use "independent prep".
- Exam content NDA: teach skills, don't copy questions.

### 2. "Redirect $1,700 of your taxes" — donor engine for the new federal scholarship credit — 8.5/10 · DFY, sell now

**Why**
- OBBBA's §25F gives a dollar-for-dollar federal credit of up to $1,700 per person ($3,400 joint) for gifts to Scholarship Granting Organisations (SGOs). It starts **1 January 2027**.
- 30–31 states had opted in by October 2026, and donors anywhere can give to SGOs in those states.
- Treasury projects 600–700 SGOs and about $26B a year in giving by 2030. The 90% spending rule leaves about 10% for operations and marketing.
- Searches are up 4×. YouTube explainers total about 8.6k views, so there are almost no donor-facing funnels.
- Sources:
  - https://www.irs.gov/government-entities/federal-state-local-governments/federal-scholarship-tax-credit-fstc-participating-states
  - https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/02/2026-20264/federal-scholarship-tax-credit
  - https://excelined.org/2026/10/05/federal-scholarship-tax-credit-rules-what-they-mean-for-states-parents-and-schools/

**Offer: "SGO Donor Engine", $15–40k plus retainer**
- Tax-credit calculator funnel.
- Compliant receipting.
- Donor CRM.
- Employer/payroll giving kit.
- Campaigns for church, parish and day-school networks.
- Meta and Google ads to high-tax ZIP codes.
- December and tax-season sequences.

**Buyers:** new SGOs, dioceses, Christian and Jewish school networks, private-school associations. A lighter $5–15k version for individual schools.

**Pairs with:** Texas TEFA and other education-savings-account states, where enrolment funnels for private schools and microschools serve the same buyers.

**Ease:** no licence for a vendor. **The window is the next 8 weeks.**

**Risks**
- Rules are still proposed.
- State opt-outs.
- Fundraising-solicitation rules.
- Politically charged.

### 4. Tariff refund intake engine — 8/10 · time-boxed

**Why**
- The Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs on 20 February 2026. About $166B is refundable across roughly 330k importers.
- CBP's refund portal opened its third phase on 6 October 2026, **for importers in litigation only**.
- The Court of International Trade certified a class of importers on 8 October 2026; the government is appealing.
- Protective-suit deadlines start in February 2027. Small importers are under-served.
- Searches are up 10× year on year, and YouTube videos drew 1.57M views.
- Sources:
  - https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/03/tariff-refund-mechanism-takes-shape
  - https://www.dwt.com/insights/2026/10/cit-certifies-ieepa-duty-refund-importer-class
  - https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/09/cbp-confirms-october-6-2026-launch-of-phase-3-of-the-ieepa-tariff-refund-process/

**Offer: "Tariff Recovery Intake Engine" for a licensed customs broker or trade-law firm, $15–30k**
- Importer list outbound.
- An AI refund estimator that reads uploaded ACE reports.
- CRM and e-sign.

Alternative: run the intake brand yourself and sell qualified cases to the partner, wherever fee-sharing rules allow.

**Ease:** live in 2–3 weeks.

**Risks**
- The window closes during 2027, and appeals could change it.
- Legal-advertising rules.
- Scammer stigma, so use radical transparency.

- YouTube "how to start" content is tiny (4.7k views at most).
- Sources:
