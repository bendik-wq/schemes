# Viral Coach: transcripts of its live Facebook/Instagram ads

Pulled 1–2 Nov 2026 from Meta's Ad Library. This is an internal swipe file for studying structure; don't copy their claims or guarantees.

## How "top performing" was decided

Meta doesn't show impressions or spend for US ads, so performance had to be judged from behaviour:

1. **Viral Coach relaunches its entire ad set every week.**
   - All 30 live ads on the Daniel Iles page started between 26 and 30 Oct.
   - On 25 Oct a different set of 28 was live (started 12–21 Oct).
   - Whatever survives the weekly cut is what's working.
2. **The ad copy never changes.**
   - The same primary text ran in every ad on both 25 Oct and 1 Nov.
   - That makes it their proven control copy.
   - Only the video, and sometimes the headline, is tested.
3. **Winning video concepts get re-cut.** They re-film the same hook and script in several versions (generic, lawyer, dentist, chiropractor, med spa). The more live variants a concept has, the more it is working.

Concepts below are ranked by number of live variants. All 30 videos were downloaded and transcribed with Whisper (small.en). Obvious mishearings are fixed (e.g. "Hermosi" → Hormozi); everything else is verbatim, so expect small transcription slips.

### The repeating script skeleton

Almost every winner follows the same structure:

1. **Hook:** a guarantee, a stat, or a callout to a pain ("word of mouth and referrals").
2. **Proof stat:** "our *average* client gets 15.5M views in 3 months; that's the average, not the best."
3. **"Give me 15 seconds and I'll explain."**
4. **3-step mechanism:**
   1. A custom strategy for your niche.
   2. You film 1–2 hours a month.
   3. They place a trained editor or social media manager in your business, plus an "organic content funnel."
5. **Low-effort promise:** "all you have to do is show up and film."
6. **Qualifier:** "we can't do this for just anyone / professionals only / there's a catch."
7. **CTA:** "Click the link below, a 10-second application, a free call with my team."

**Risk reversals they rotate:**
- 1M views or you don't pay.
- First month free.
- 7-day trial for attorneys.
- A "performance guarantee, so we have no reason to sell you."
