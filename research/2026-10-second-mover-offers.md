# Second-mover offers: where demand is proven and a $15–50k done-for-you setup sells

Research date: 25 October 2026. Prepared for Bendik / Asym.

**The play:** find a market where demand is exploding and a first mover has proven the model. Then come in second ("ladder to the first mover") with the compliant, productised, better-built done-for-you (DFY) setup, sold at $15–50k to buyers who already have capital, a licence, an audience or a business.

## How this was researched

- **Google Trends** (US, 5 years and 12 months). 105 keywords across health, finance, AI and commerce, each measured against the same anchor term ("med spa") so groups are comparable. Plus "rising" related searches for 20 seed terms. Raw data: `research/data/google-trends-*.json`. Tool: `research/tools/harvest.py`.
- **YouTube search** (50 topics, first page of results). Views and upload age per video, used to measure where attention is going right now. Raw data: `research/data/youtube-search-2026-10-25.json`. Tool: `research/tools/yt.py`.
- **Instagram and TikTok.** Instagram is login-walled, so it came via secondary sources. TikTok came via articles and platform reports (TikTok started blocking peptide searches in Oct 2026).
- **Four research tracks** (health/longevity, finance/financing, AI/commerce/local, plus the Trends and YouTube pulls). They covered news, regulators (FDA, FTC, CFPB, state AGs, DEA, SEC), funding rounds, franchise disclosure documents and the first movers' actual offers and prices. Sources are cited inline.

Numbers marked *est.* are estimates; *vendor claim* means unverified marketing figures. Every DFY price below is a proposed price, not a market quote.

## 3. The top five in detail

### #1 Peptide-Ready Clinic Launch — $35–45k, 45–60 days

**Your example, done the way that survives.** The "done-for-you peptide business" most YouTube coaches sell is a **research-use-only (RUO) store**. That is now a federal target:
- FDA warning letters went to five RUO sellers on 24 Aug 2026. The FDA ignored the "not for human use" disclaimers and inferred human use from the bacteriostatic water and dosing calculators on the sites.
- The owner of Paradigm Peptides was sentenced to about 6 years in prison.
- Peptide Sciences (an estimated $7M+ a month) shut down in March.
- Stripe, PayPal and Shopify block peptides, and TikTok now blocks peptide searches.

The legal version has perfect timing:
- On 15 Apr 2026 the FDA took 12 peptides off its "do not compound" list.
- In July an advisory committee voted to allow BPC-157, TB-500, KPV and others for compounding.
- An interim access policy has been at the White House since about 16 Oct 2026.
- Hims & Hers has said it will sell already-permitted peptides by year-end and BPC-157 after FDA action.

**Sources:** frierlevitt.com/articles/fda-peptides-do-not-compound-list-update-2026 · americanmedspa.org/news/fda-peptide-interim-access-policy-white-house-review · peptidecareindex.com/research/fda-peptide-warning-letters

**First movers:**
- **Telehealth platforms** (tech and prescribers only): OpenLoop (about $9k setup + $1.5k/mo + revenue share), Telegra, Cuvo, Karpa.
- **RUO store builders** (PeptideSetup, $699–1,499/mo).
- **Clinician courses** (from $2,495).

None of them builds the brand, offer, funnel, compliant ads, CRM and retention.

**Deliverables:**
- MSO/PC structure via a partner healthcare attorney.
- Telehealth partner with a 50-state prescriber network and two 503A pharmacies.
- **Formulary that is legal today:** sermorelin, NAD+, glutathione, PT-141, non-injectable GHK-Cu, branded GLP-1, and a lab panel.
- A "coming soon" waitlist for BPC-157, TB-500 and the rest, switched on the day the policy lands.
- LegitScript application and a high-risk merchant account plus a backup.
- HIPAA-aware storefront with subscriptions.
- Claims-safe copy and ad library.
- AI intake and refill agents.
- Server-side tracking.

**Guarantee:** "Live and taking paid patients in 60 days, or we keep working free and refund 25%." Never guarantee revenue.

**Buyers:**
- Fitness and biohacking creators with 50k–1M followers.
- Gym and supplement brand owners.
- **RUO sellers who need to go legitimate after the August letters** — the most motivated buyers.
- NPs, PAs and doctors who want a cash-pay brand.

**Hook:** "Hims is waiting for one White House signature. Be live before they are."

**Rules:**
- Charge a flat fee. Never take a percentage of patient revenue, because of fee-splitting and anti-kickback laws.
- Never call a compounded drug "generic" or "the same as" an approved drug.
- The business must be profitable on today's formulary alone, in case the policy is delayed.

### #2 Finance-First Sales System for contractors — $18k + $2–3k/mo, 30 days

**"Instant financing", correctly structured:**
- Licensed platforms (Wisetack, Hearth, Cherry, Sunbit, GreenSky) are the lenders.
- **You are paid by the merchant.** Never take per-loan fees from lenders on consumer loans: that is loan brokering and needs a licence (e.g. California CFL).

**Evidence:**
- ACCA 2026 survey: contractors who offer financing close 49% of jobs vs 38%. 32% offer none, and only 28% lead with a monthly payment.
- Wisetack has 40k contractor merchants. LendingClub entered this market in Nov 2025.
- The platforms sell software and trainers sell courses. **Nobody installs the system end to end.**

**Deliverables:**
- 3+ lender waterfall (prime → second-look → lease-to-own).
- Soft-pull pre-qualification link in the CRM, quotes, website and SMS.
- Monthly-payment proposal templates and rep scripts.
- Ads with Reg Z disclosures.
- AI follow-up on approved-but-unclosed quotes and on declines ("second look").
- Dashboard: application rate, approval rate, financed share, ticket size.

**Guarantee:** "Live in 30 days with 3+ lenders." Never guarantee approvals.

**Buyer:** HVAC, roofing, remodelling, windows and pool contractors doing $2–20M with ticket sizes of $8k+.

**Hook:** "32% of contractors don't offer financing — and close 11 points fewer deals."

**Avoid:**
- Solar.
- Dealer fees hidden in the price. The Michigan AG sued over an average hidden fee of 27%.

**Variants:**
- Patient financing (#5) for med spas and dental.
- A checkout and financing stack for coaches. Only for coaches with documented outcomes: no card stacking, no "every customer approved" claims.

## 4. Kill list: looks hot, is a trap

1. **"Research peptide" (RUO) store setups.** FDA warning letters ignored the disclaimers; there are prison sentences, payment bans and TikTok search blocks. **Redirect these buyers into #1.**
2. **Mass-market compounded GLP-1 brands.** Three waves of FDA warning letters, branded pills at $149/mo, and Hims retreating to branded drugs. Only viable as a branded service line (#6).
3. **Credit-card-stacking "funding agencies" and "business funding to pay for coaching".**
   - FTC v. Seek Capital: $48.3M judgment and a lifetime ban; Seed Consulting is an earlier case.
   - Credit repair can't charge upfront fees (CROA and the Telemarketing Sales Rule).
   - **The legitimate version is #8.**
4. **THC/hemp drinks.** The federal ban on intoxicating hemp products takes effect 11 Dec 2026.
5. **Prediction markets / Kalshi affiliates and crypto ATMs.** Kalshi searches are up 25×, but about 20 states are litigating, Arizona has filed criminal charges, and several states ban crypto ATMs.
6. **"Start your AI automation / AI receptionist agency" sold to beginners.**
   - Search interest has collapsed (0.1× year on year).
   - DFY bundles sell for $497–1,799.
   - The FTC banned Click Profit and Ecommerce Empire Builders for income-claim "DFY business" sales.
   - **Only sell AI practices to people who already have distribution (#9).**
7. **Residential solar, EV chargers, exosomes/stem cells, ABA therapy, express car washes:**
   - Solar and EV chargers lost their federal credits, and the largest installer filed for Chapter 11.
   - Exosome and stem-cell clinics get FDA warning letters.
   - ABA therapy faces Medicaid rate cuts.
   - Express car washes are overbuilt.

## 5. Rules that apply to every offer

- **Sell to buyers who already have something:** capital, a licence, an audience, a business or a deadline. The FTC enforces against "business-in-a-box" sold to beginners.
- **Guarantee deliverables and dates, never income, revenue, patients, approvals or funding.** No earnings claims in ads.
- **Charge flat fees.**
  - No percentage of patient revenue (fee-splitting / anti-kickback).
  - No per-loan fees on consumer credit (broker licensing).
  - No success fees on capital raised (broker-dealer rules).
- **A partner attorney does the legal work.** Asym builds the business and never gives legal advice.
- **Build compliance into the product.** That is the second-mover edge: the first movers proved demand by cutting corners, and you win by being the one that survives.
