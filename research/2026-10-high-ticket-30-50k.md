# Offers that can easily carry $30–50k (price-validated)

Research date: 25 October 2026. Prepared for Bendik / Asym.

**The test:** a $30–50k done-for-you fee is easy only when all four hold:
1. The buyer already has money.
2. The setup is worth at least **5–10× the fee** in year one, shown with real numbers.
3. A comparable price already exists (franchise fees, consultants, legal/advisory fees).
4. There is a reason to buy now (a deadline or a market shift).

Every candidate from `2026-10-niche-tailwinds.md` and `2026-10-second-mover-offers.md` was pressure-tested on this test, and a fresh discovery pass looked for new $30k+ offers. Estimates are labelled.

## The pattern

$30–50k sells easily in three situations:

- **The work raises enterprise value.** A recurring-revenue book sells at 2–12× its revenue or earnings to private-equity buyers.
- **A deadline opens new money.** A 1 Jan 2027 start date and a "be live first" pitch.
- **The buyer adds a high-margin service line.** Each new patient, client or member is worth thousands a year.

Consumer and hobby niches (mahjong, decluttering, flag football, driver test prep) don't carry this price. Run those yourself or sell them at $5–15k.

## ✅ Easy at $30–50k

### 1. Founding Donor Engine: scholarship tax credit SGOs — $40k (+ $5k/mo through tax season)
- **Why now:**
  - The federal §25F credit starts **1 Jan 2027**: up to $1,700 per person, dollar for dollar, in 30–31 states.
  - Treasury projects about $26B a year across 600–700 SGOs by 2030, roughly $40M each on average.
  - The 90/10 rule funds marketing.
- **Buyer value (est.):** a diocesan SGO raising 2,000 donors × $1,700 = $3.4M in year one. The fee equals about 24 donors, and donors renew every year.
- **Price anchors:** fundraising consultants charge $2.5–15k a month; feasibility studies cost $30–70k.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Credit calculator and donor site.
  - Segregated receipting.
  - CRM with parish and school attribution.
  - Ambassador kits for parishes and schools.
  - National donor ads (donors can give out of state).
  - Year-end and tax-season sequences.
  - CPA and adviser outreach.
  - A 90/10 dashboard.
- **Sell to:** dioceses (5,829 Catholic schools), Christian and Jewish school networks, national SGOs.
- **Hook:** "From 1 January every taxpayer can redirect $1,700 to your schools."
- **Compliance:**
  - Register as fundraising counsel in about 26 states (budget $5–10k a year).
  - Flat fee only, never a percentage of money raised.
  - No tax advice.
- **⏰ Window:** sell in the next 6–8 weeks.

### 2. "Select-Ready in 90 Days": Claude Partner practice launch — $45k

  - Only employees of partner organisations can take the exams.
  - Sources: anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network · anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub
- **Buyer value (est.):** IT service providers (MSPs) and dev shops with 20–200 staff. Clients pay $25–150k per LLM project; 4 projects plus 2 retainers come to about $260k in year one.

  - Partner application.
  - Certification bootcamp for 10 staff.
  - 3 productised Claude offers.
  - **Co-delivery of the buyer's first 2 production deployments.** This is what makes it worth $45k.

- **Prerequisite:** Asym joins the network and certifies its own team first.
- **Upsell:** fractional practice lead at $8–15k a month.

### 3. "PE-Ready" builds for roll-up industries — $40–45k
- **Same play, two verticals:**
  - **Fire and life-safety inspection operators.** Recurring books sell at 2–3.5× revenue. PE-backed buyers include Pye-Barker, Blackstone and KKR.
  - **HVAC, plumbing and electrical.** These trade at about 9.5× EBITDA. A 40%+ recurring share drives the multiple: Champions Group sold to Blackstone at about 18.5×.
- **Buyer value (est.):**
  - Inspection: 150 new recurring accounts ≈ $90k recurring revenue ≈ **$180–315k added enterprise value**.
  - HVAC: +0.5× on $1.5M EBITDA ≈ **$750k**.
- **Price anchors:**
  - A HOODZ franchise costs a $60k fee plus a 7–10% royalty.
  - Sell-side advisory runs $80–250k.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Maintenance-membership or inspection-agreement programme.
  - Outbound plus local search engine.
  - CRM with deficiency-to-quote and auto-renewal.
  - AI booking.
  - A recurring-revenue and EBITDA dashboard in the format PE buyers diligence.
  - Owner-independence SOPs.
  - A data room ready for a quality-of-earnings review, done with a CPA.
- **Guarantee:** e.g. 75 new recurring accounts in 90 days, or Asym keeps working free.
- **Buyers:** about 120k HVAC contractors; fire-equipment licensees from public state lists; owners aged 55+.
- **Channel:** M&A advisors and exit planners as referral partners.
- **Hook:** "Same EBITDA, two different multiples."

### 5. OZ 2.0 Ready-to-Raise: Opportunity Zone fund launch — $45k (+ $6–10k/mo)

### 7. Coverage Shield for community health centres (FQHCs) — $45k (+ $6k/mo) · slow to close

## Rules for every high-ticket offer
- **Flat fees only.** No percentage of funds raised, patients, policies, refunds or capital raised. Those breach healthcare anti-kickback rules, fundraising ethics, attorney fee-sharing rules and securities rules.
- **Guarantee delivery, never outcomes.** Promise "live by X / N meetings booked, or we keep working free", not raises, approvals or valuations.
- **Payment terms:**
  - 50% at kickoff and 50% at go-live, or
  - $25–30k upfront plus a monthly retainer.
- **Partners do the licensed work:** attorneys, CPAs, customs brokers and revenue-cycle firms. Asym builds the machine.
