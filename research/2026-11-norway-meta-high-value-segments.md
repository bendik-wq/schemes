These are Monid Meta Ads Library scans of Norway, the 40 most-seen active ads per query. Raw summaries are in `data/norway-meta-2026-11-01/`. Ad counts per advertiser are a floor (only the top 40 ads per query are sampled), not total spend.

So the money in oil, shipping and industry on Meta goes to **recruiting skilled workers**. Tools like Hyr and Jobbnorge already sell this, so buyers are proven to pay.

## Where to charge a lot with pay per lead / pay per result

1. **Skilled-worker recruitment for shipyards, offshore and industry.** This is the oil and shipping angle.
   - **Buyers:** shipyards (Vard, Ulstein, Westcon, Myklebust and others), offshore service companies, electrical and automation contractors.
   - **Evidence:** Hyr, Jobbnorge and Caera ads; Østensjø hiring.
   - **Value:** an agency hire costs about 15–20% of a NOK 600–750k salary, so NOK 90–150k.
   - **Our price:**
     - setup NOK 60–100k (employer-brand film, landing pages, screening);
     - plus **NOK 30–50k per hire** or NOK 3–5k per qualified, screened candidate.
   - **Fit:** we already have the funnel, scoring and AI-call stack.
2. **Buyer engine for housing and cabin developers.** Biggest ticket.
   - **Value:** marketing typically takes 1–3% of project value.
   - **Our price:**
     - NOK 150–300k per project;
     - plus NOK 1.5–3k per qualified viewing or registration.
   - **Legal check:** a success fee per *sale* may count as brokerage under eiendomsmeglingsloven. Charge per qualified lead and viewing, not per sale, unless a lawyer approves.
3. **Pay per booked consultation for private clinics** (implants, cosmetic dentistry, eye surgery outside the big chains).
   - **Price:** NOK 1.5–4k per booked consultation, or a retainer.
   - **Legal check:** health marketing rules (helsepersonelloven §13, markedsføringsloven) limit before/after images and promises.

## Caveats
- Norway is small. Ad counts here are tens, not the hundreds or thousands seen in the US. That suggests little competition, but also a lower ceiling.
- Meta's housing and employment ad categories restrict targeting by age, gender and postcode in many markets. Plan for broad targeting with creative-led qualification.
