# Northbid: tender & RFQ engine for offshore suppliers

**Promise:** Read every RFQ in minutes and bid only on the ones you can win. Installed for you in 14 days.

**Buyer:** offshore suppliers (subsea, topside, marine, wind, services), 10–500 staff, NOK 20M+ revenue, 3+ RFQs a month, bids written by engineers / MD / sales rather than a bid department. Qualified in Magnet JQS or Achilles, or getting there.

**Two outcomes:** SAVE (senior engineering hours per bid, missed clauses, expired certificates) · GROW (more bids answered, better go/no-go, cleaner submissions).

## Value stack
| Component | What |
|---|---|
| Workspace | RFQ reader, NORSOK-aware compliance matrix, bid/no-bid score, CSV export, tender watch (TED + UK Find a Tender) |
| Library build | Up to 60 approved entries from the client's documents (ISO/NORSOK evidence, HSE stats, Z-018 practice, Transparency Act statement, standard terms) |
| First three RFQs run together | Live bids, matrix to submission |
| Training + monthly tuning | Two sessions; monthly library and ranking review |
