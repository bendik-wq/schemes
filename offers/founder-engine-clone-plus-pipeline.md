## Why not the two ideas on their own

**A clone on its own can't hold $20–50k.**
- HeyGen makes a twin from about 15 seconds of footage for $29–149 a month. It passed $200M ARR in June 2026.
- Done-for-you clone setups on the market cost $995 one-time to about $2.5k a month.
- No public offer anywhere comes close to a $20k setup.
- Prospects will google it, and the anchor they find is about $30 a month.
- An honest "4K, indistinguishable from real" claim isn't possible yet. Long takes, hands and body motion give it away, and Norwegian lip sync is weak.
- From 2 Aug 2026, EU AI Act Art. 50 requires a label on deepfakes. YouTube, TikTok, Meta and LinkedIn label AI video automatically through C2PA.

**Rev-share lead gen on its own isn't mass market.**
- It works one vertical at a time. Biggest US vertical demand relative to "lead generation" (1.0):

  | Vertical | Size | Change |
  |---|---|---|
  | dental marketing | 0.34 | flat |
  | insurance leads | 0.22 | +21% |
  | law firm marketing | 0.20 | +27% |
  | med spa | 0.03 | +57% |

- That makes it a good business, but not one that sells to everyone.

## What does sell at $20–50k
Price follows the outcome. $30k+ sells easily when the work **adds revenue**: a new client is worth thousands. So sell revenue to people who already sell a high-ticket offer, and use the clone and the funnel stack as the means.

**Buyer:** founders, coaches, consultants, agency owners and B2B service firms with an offer of $3k+ and existing sales calls. A large market:
- Viral Coach claims 3,000+ clients.
- Atom Acquisition has a $30k+ tier.
- Cole Gordon, Leadscape and Modrn all scale on Meta in this segment.

**Promise:** "You film once. We run your content, funnel and booked calls. You only take the calls."

**What's in it:**
1. **Capture day (2 h)** → licensed clone (video + voice) with written consent, labelled per EU/platform rules.
2. **Content engine:**
   - 30 shorts and 4 long videos a month in their voice.
   - Clone footage mixed with real clips from capture day.
   - Scripts from their own calls and wins, which they approve.
3. **Pipeline system.** Already built in `funnel/` (Worker + D1, VSL, application scoring, booking, email/voice follow-up, Funnel HQ CRM):
   - Paid and organic traffic.
   - VSL → application → booked call.
   - Attribution down to cash collected.
4. **Setter / speed-to-lead** (AI calls with consent + human setters).
5. **Monthly creative refresh:** 15–30 new ads a month. The ad research shows the winners do this.

**Price (proposal):**
- **Core:** $25k setup + $5k/month (content + funnel + ads management).
- **Partner:** $15k setup + 10–15% of attributable cash collected for 12 months. Funnel HQ already tracks first-touch attribution to payments, which is what makes rev share enforceable.
- **Founding:** first 5 clients −20% in exchange for case-study rights.

**Guarantee (written, deliverable-based):**
- Content volume delivered every month or that month is free.
- The clone is approved by the client before the balance is due.
- Funnel live in 21 days.
- No follower or revenue guarantees.

**Why it wins:**
- Viral Coach-style offers still need the founder on camera every week. "Film once" removes the reason those clients churn.
- AI-video demand is surging:
  - "ai video generator" is 3.2× "lead generation" and up 76% on last year.
  - "heygen" is up 154%.
  - "personal branding" is up 135%.
  - "appointment setting" is up 96%.
- "AI automation agency" is cooling (−14%). Don't lead with automation.

**Rules we follow:**
- Client's own likeness only, ID-verified, written scope and revocation, delete the model on request.
- On-screen AI label + C2PA.
- The clone never gives testimonials or endorsements.
- Every script approved by the client.
- DPA for biometric data (GDPR).
