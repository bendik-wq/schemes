# How to use this document

**What it is.** One hundred ready-to-film scripts. Every episode teaches one idea about buying, growing and selling businesses with little or none of your own money, then sells one thing: a call with Josh.

**Who it's for.** People who want to make money from owning businesses: owners of $1–20M businesses, high earners who want equity instead of salary, and people with capital looking for better returns than property or shares.

**How each episode is laid out.**

| Part | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| Header table | The money angle, the first line, who it's for, the stage, runtime and copy structure |
| Script | Labelled beats with times: Hook, Stakes, Frame, Soft CTA, the numbered parts, The Numbers, Where People Lose Money, Close |
| On screen | Text overlays and disclaimers, shown as indented callouts |
| Production | Three titles, thumbnail text, three Shorts, the description and the pinned comment |

**Gaps for Josh.** Anything only Josh knows is marked **[JOSH: …]**. All of them are collected in the appendix as one interview sheet.

**Navigation.** In Google Docs, open the outline (View → Show outline) to jump to any act or episode.

# How the scripts are built

**The through-line.** Credibility → Capital → Closing → Grow → Exit. The three Cs get you the business. Grow raises its profit and its multiple. Exit is where the money is made.

**The format.** Each episode borrows the way the best operator-educators on YouTube hook and explain: the number first, no greeting, the whole idea in one line, then a labelled breakdown (levels, mistakes, X vs Y, or a teardown), one worked example, and a decision rule. The pitch comes late, short and narrow.

**The persuasion layer.** Alen Sultanic's frameworks: one opener type per episode, a not-your-fault line, one belief shift, then two paths, an identity close and the 30-days-from-now close.

**Rules that never bend.**

- Josh's stories and numbers come only from his own record, with relationships stated exactly: Cam, Jason and Elliot are friends; Jy is a student; Saul is a member; Christian Dawes is an example.
- "Results not typical; records on file" sits next to every deal or income figure. "Education, not financial or legal advice" sits on every structuring, tax or legal point. Worked examples are labelled illustrative.
- No price, no fake scarcity, no income promises.

**Publishing.** Two episodes a week, in order: 50 weeks for all 100. Judge each video by booked calls, not views. Every description link carries its own `utm_content=epNN`.
