# "The 3C Machine": a 40-video YouTube series that sells the 3C acquisition model

Every episode is a VSL for one thing: **a call with Josh about buying a business with little to no money down** (Owners Academy or 1:1 advisory with G&L M&A Advisory). Content comes from two sources:

1. **The 3C model** as Josh teaches it: [source-owners-academy.md](source-owners-academy.md) (offer, proof bank, objections, masterclass teaching points) and the `3c-acquisition-model` skill (`~/.claude/skills/synced/*/3c-acquisition-model/SKILL.md` + `references/`).
2. **Daniel Iles' playbook** ([playbook.md](../../research/daniel-iles/playbook.md), 90 of his videos): what he teaches about offers, cash, sales, teams, owner-independence and "boring" businesses, applied to *buying* and *growing* a business. Credited by name with timestamped links.

The earlier 20-video series for the funnel build is kept in [../the-machine/](../the-machine/).

## 2. The red thread: the 3C Machine

One line runs through all 40: **Credibility → Capital → Closing → Grow → Exit.** The first three Cs get you the business. Daniel Iles' lessons are how you grow it and make it worth more. The roll-up is how it pays off.

- Every episode opens on the 3C map with its stage lit: "This is Capital, episode 3 of 5."
- Every episode teaches its piece well enough to act on alone.
- Every episode turns the same way: "You can do all of this alone. Most people who do it alone take years and lose deals. Or you can do it with me."
- Every episode hands off to the next.
- **Why Daniel fits:** he built a $5M/month business and teaches what makes a business valuable: price, profit, cash discipline, a sales machine, and a company that runs without its owner. Those are exactly the levers a buyer pulls after closing, and what the next buyer pays a higher multiple for.

## 3. Rules for every script

1. **Josh's claims and stories come only from `source-owners-academy.md`**, with the relationship as stated (friend ≠ student). No invented students, deals or numbers. Where new proof will go: `[PROOF: …]`.
2. **Disclaimers:** deal-size and income claims carry "results not typical; records on file" on screen. Structuring, tax and legal content carries "education, not financial or legal advice". Description says "Not affiliated with Daniel Iles or Viral Coach."
3. **Daniel's numbers stay his**, credited with the timestamp link from the playbook or video notes. Never invent a Daniel quote, number or link.
4. **Daniel's VSL timing:** what we do, why it's different, proof and the CTA inside ~3 minutes (soft pitch ~2:30). Lesson starts by 0:45. Say who it's not for, more than once. One CTA.
5. **Voice:** Josh — direct, calm, a bit blunt ("decide at 60%"), plain English, Australian. No hype words.
6. **Ad-safe hooks:** no personal-attribute call-outs, no income claim in the first line.
7. **Length:** 10–13 minutes spoken (≈1,500–1,900 words).

## 6. Publishing and measurement

- Two videos a week in order (20 weeks). If the start is slow, pull 11 (speed run), 02 (why sellers say yes) and 36 (no money) forward: each stands alone.
- Playlists per act; every end-screen points to 01.
- Judge each video by applications and booked calls, not views. Every link carries its own `utm_content`.
- Cut every episode's three best hooks into Shorts and ads.
- Tag lost calls by reason (money, time, credibility, timing). Each quarter, re-cut and pin the episode that answers the top reason.
