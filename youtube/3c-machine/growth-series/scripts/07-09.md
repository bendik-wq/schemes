And here's what most owners miss about the money side. A buyer who pays over time can often give a seller a better outcome than a buyer with cash. One illustrative way to look at it. The fourth piece is how it's paid for.

The consolidator in your head has a war chest. The real one mostly has a structure.

Each business is bought with debt sized to its own profit, at one and a half times cover, plus the seller being paid over time.
