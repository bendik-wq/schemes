## What each script does

**One bottleneck, decompiled.** Name the bottleneck the owner feels, in their words. Concede what's true about it. Then take it apart into 3–5 pieces, one distinction at a time, until the viewer sees that the ceiling isn't effort but structure. Then show acquisition breaking it.

**Brain centres, in a deliberate order.** Follow the psywar trigger map.

- **Open on the low point and the loss the owner already feels.** That's the threat system.
- **Open a loop for curiosity.**
- **Bound the fear early with safety:** the worst case in numbers, and the walls around it.
- **Build anticipation with a mechanism revealed in order:** discover, name, demonstrate.
- **Bring in story and a face:** Josh's own, or the sourced friends and students, with relationships exact.
- **Use a peak moment:** the flip and the "aha" analogy.
- **Then logic:** the numbers, and a test to run tonight.
- **End on a calm, still close that shuts the loop the hook opened.**

**Status is shown, never sold.** Use the script's status picture from the map as one or two concrete scenes:

- the industry dinner where people come to you;
- the retiring owner who rings you first;
- the board meeting you chair;
- the banker who calls you;
- the group you hand to the kids.

"Show the doing. Never narrate the having." Never luxury, envy, cars, income flexing or putting peers down. Write it so the viewer quietly places themselves at the level above where they are now. The identity close names that level: "You're the kind of owner who...".

**Massive growth in proportion.** Use the landing-page promise ("an acquisition that can double or triple the business") and illustrative numbers, always labelled as illustrative. No income promises, no guaranteed outcomes. Pair growth with the 1.5× safety rule so it reads as disciplined ambition, not hype.

## Rules

- **Length:** 2,900–3,400 spoken words.
- **Analogies:** 4–5 per script, with one hero analogy and its callback.
- **Analogies you may not use:** each script's analogies must be fresh. Do **not** reuse any analogy from the belief-shift scripts. Search `belief-content/scripts/*.md` before choosing. Banned for this series: towing capacity, orchard vs seedlings, workshop hoist, bridge load limit, warehouse racking, builder's contingency line, the ladder and the leak, house plans, a third of your house, prime mover, the family-home sale, the house auction, four houses sold to a developer, owner-driver with one truck. Analogies must also be unique across this series. Your three scripts must not share any.
- **Standalone:** never mention other videos, a series, or "next time". You may use the phrase "the growth ceiling".
- **Voice:** Josh's. Calm, Australian, direct. No exclamation marks, no hype, no fake urgency.
- **Invitation:** one soft link mention about a third of the way in. Close on the exact invitation sentence from `belief-content/script-brief.md`. The 7-day refund at most once in each of your three scripts, and in only one of them.
- **Facts:** no new facts. Illustrative scenes and numbers must read as illustrative.
- **Output format:** the same as before. `# N. Title`, then plain paragraphs, no markdown inside. Titles are strong YouTube titles about growth (e.g. "Why Your Business Stopped Growing at $3 Million (And It Isn't Your Marketing)").
