## 1. Growth: what he believes

### 1.1 Why people stay stuck

**Each level has a ceiling.** Getting better at your current level doesn't break through it.
- "Each level has a limitation of income and influence. And no matter how good you get, you'll hit that limitation sooner or later." (Oct 29, 2024)

**People get stuck at their own competence.**
- "Many in the game never have this realization and spend their entire journey being stuck at what they're really good at and making everyone else around them richer." (Nov 18)

**Doing more of the same runs into a resistance curve.**
- "more of the same hits a point of diminishing returns, and you'll hit this resistance curve where it costs more and more, and it's harder and harder to get more of the same. That's when the business will plateau, and they get stuck." (Nov 6)

**Cutting and saving can't produce growth.**
- "It's nearly impossible to grow a business or grow personally by decreasing costs and trying to "save money." There is a bottom to it." (Dec 7)

**Fear of loss holds people back.**
- "The real question isn't what we need to do to get ahead, but more so what's holding us from doing it in the first place. And that something is fear." (Nov 15)

**The people around you set the ceiling.**
- "Sociology tends to rule psychology". He says you can "bring them up to your level" or "bring yourself down to their level", and the first "is nearly impossible", so "This is where all of your self-sabotage starts to come in." (Oct 15)

**Decision-making is the founder's bottleneck.**
- "I find that the biggest bottle neck to growth is decision making and the biggest bottle neck when it comes to decision making is spending money." (Feb 6)

### 1.2 What creates big jumps

**Transcend the level rather than polishing it.**
- "The faster you can transcend the level you're at, the faster and more money you'll make." (Oct 29)
- "Notice how each one of these levels hires and pays the lower levels, and the only reason they can do that is because the lower levels make the higher levels more money." (Oct 29)
- His example is a re-titling: "from selling his services as "a media buyer", which were limited in income, to "a CMO who can buy media"". (Oct 29)
- The question he leaves the reader with: "how do I transcend this level?" (Oct 29)

**Income moves in jumps, not in a line.**
- "This game is all about non-linear income. You don't get incremental raises like you do having a job. Instead, you have income jumps". (Feb 19)
- "keep an eye out for what's NOT working instead of what is working. What's not working is what's holding you back from making those big jumps." (Oct 26)

**Shorten the timeline. He calls this leverage.**
- "on a long enough timeline anything is possible" → "How do I shorten that timeline so that it's possible now? [...] Who created it so I don't have to and can use it now? [...] It's ALL about shortening the timeline. That's the real nature of leverage". (May 2)

**Look for the people not yet giving you money.**
- "The key to making a lot of money in the game isn't to focus on who's already giving you money but rather on those who don't give you money because that's where all the growth is." (Nov 6)
- His example is Amazon going from books to music, then electronics, then AWS and Whole Foods: "Who else isn't giving us money? That's the question every massive company asks and how they expand." (Nov 6)

**Other people are the multiplier.**
- "The difference between millionaires and billionaires is that [...] they also focus on having others be smart, hardworking, and having good timing." (Aug 14)

### 1.3 Economics and leverage

**Scale and growth are different things.**
- "Scale is when you grow revenue without having to add resources to support that revenue." (Oct 8)
- "Growth is when you grow revenue by adding resources to support that revenue. Hence when you're "growing", you're growing "everything". This is why team size follows revenue". (Oct 8)

**The market sets the ceiling.**
- "the #1 thing that limits scale is the market's ability to absorb the scale you're trying to place onto it. Markets determine the limits of scale." (Oct 8)
- "It's like putting in a 5-star restaurant with the best marketing in a tiny town. Not enough hungry ppl to eat." (Oct 8)
- "90% market you're in, 10% offer you have." (Jul 11)

**Money over time.**
- "a customer is a renewable financial resource [...] not just acquiring "customers" but rather acquiring "revenue"." (Feb 27) (CG)

**Product beats talent.**
- "You can resell the product a million times and yet can't resell talent a million times. [...] Product > talent = scale." (Sep 1)

**Time is the first limit.**
- "Automate + delegate = scale." (Aug 18)
- "Time is the first limit of the scale. [...] Once you can scale time, the rest is simply the management of complexity via rules and structures." (Aug 18)

**Count money in the bank, not top-line revenue.**
- "That huge exit someone had? What are the terms? What was the take-home?" (Dec 9) (CG)

### 1.4 Buying, owning and building

**Own rather than serve.**
- "The only way to get rich in the game is by running/owning offers. You'll make good money doing other stuff but won't get rich." (Jan 4)
- From the P.S.: "that "other stuff" you do is what makes others rich. So if you can do it for them, why not do it for yourself?" (Jan 4)
- "Selling your skills gets you a nice monthly allowance. Owning the offer is where the real money is. [...] that's who gets paid 1st and then decides how much everyone else gets paid." https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/2103939524682207557 (Sep 26, 2026)

**Own the whole of it.**
- "Why have a tiny % of the pie when you can own the whole pie?" (Dec 4) (CG)
- He is rejecting performance or "phantom equity", a phrase "that sounds good but doesn't put cash in the bank." (Dec 4)

**Acquirers come from below.**
- "Volkswagen owns Bentley, Bugatti, Lamborghini, Audi, Ducati and Porche." (Jul 28)
- "the only reason high-ticket companies were able to survive was because they were acquired by low-ticket companies." (Jul 28 P.S.)
- "Patek was bought by the Stern brothers, who were their suppliers." (Dec 20). This is his closest analogue to a neighbour or supplier buying the business.

**Buying compresses time.**
- "If they had money and work ethic, they bought skills. If they had money and skills, they scaled faster than everyone else." https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/2021954088628347154 (Feb 12, 2026)
- "I personally never paid for it and always built an offer or a business to pay for anything I wanted. [...] The power of leverage." https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/1886122359767121951 (Feb 2, 2025)

### 1.5 Levels

- "$0-$5m — all about the product and marketing. $5-$10m — all about people and teams. $10+ — all about systems. This is why what gets you to one level isn't what gets you to the next." (Jul 24) (CG). **This is the strongest frame for a $1M–$20M audience.**
- His exit from a level is a set of questions:
  - "What do I lose when I move forward?"
  - "What do I gain out of losing this?"
  - "What can I do differently so I don't lose that and have both?" (Nov 15)

## 2. Status and identity

### 2.1 Levelling up and who you become

- "What do you need to let go so you can become who you want to become?" (Feb 22)
- "The 1st step to growth is knowing what you are. What you define, you confine, and what you confine you limit." (Mar 13)
- "Who would you be if you didn't __________? [...] there is a version of you that's already capable of achieving the goals you want. You just have to meet that version of you." (May 31)
- "Your mind must arrive at your destination before your life does." https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/1977371165254459847 (Oct 12, 2025)
- "Most Of You Have Million a Dollar Skillset and a $10k -$100k Mindset". The drill he gives: "who am I not to be worthy of making a million dollars?" https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/1703663857086943703 (Sep 18, 2023)

### 2.2 The room you're in

- "we are the sum average of the people we associate with". He describes paying a $20k bar tab, leaving, and "never talked to any of them ever again", then: "either we're going up or we're going down." (Oct 15)
- "everyone wants to be the "smartest guy in the room" yet no one wants to be the "hardest working guy" in the room. [...] the hardest working guys become the richest guys in the room". (May 1)
- "Look at them, don't look up at them. [...] they're just humans who had a little more courage to do what you want to do." (Jun 9)

### 2.3 Rich vs wealthy

- "How it feels to be rich. You have money and can buy almost anything but don't have time to enjoy it. How it feels to be wealthy. You can buy almost anything and have time to enjoy it. Time is a factor of wealth, whereas money is a factor of both." (Jun 1)
- "at higher levels of success the measures of success change from what you can get, buy or own to what you can give and how you can help others achieve more." (Jan 8)
- "The #1 reason you should get rich is so you can drop $20k into your mom's bank account without thinking twice about it." https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/2010716521295745215 (Jan 12, 2026)

### 2.4 Being seen: status as a commercial asset

- "Status provides trust, which saves time." (Oct 4) (CG)
- "the higher the guarantee, the lower the status." He follows it with: "you can check your status in the market place by the amount of "guarantee" the market place demands on you". (Jan 23)
- "More trophies -> more status -> more bragging rights -> more exposure in the market place -> more clients → more other services sold -> more money." (Apr 1)
- "you need to LOOK LIKE you're building Rolls-Royces [...] if you use something that's a commodity, then you by proxy also become a commodity." (Apr 11)

### 2.5 Legacy, framed as stewardship

He never uses the word "legacy" itself.
- "NHB is bigger than me, and therefore, if it's to grow, I've decided that it can no longer be about me... ...I'm no longer the star". (Sep 25)
- "the only thing left is to help others get to the level I'm at". (Nov 11)
- "my mission wasn't to make money to keep for myself, it was to make money and reinvest in this industry". https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/1879523061482860720 (Jan 15, 2025)

### 2.6 How he uses status in copy

**Subtle uses (copy the mechanism):**
1. **Permission, not prestige.** "Rolex, Patek, AP, Gucci, LV, Hermes and alike all sell permission. Not "status" or "quality" or "heritage", those are the conscious front ends, the subconscious back end is the permission." (Mar 12)
2. **Identity work only at higher price points.** "You don't pay $15,000 - $50,000 for a watch that tells time. You buy it to tell the world you've made it." Above about $3k–$10k, "it's all about identifying work [...] how others see them". At lower prices it "can backfire as it's overkill." (Nov 26)
3. **The know-you ladder.** "They know you / They like you / They admire you / They want to be like you / They want to be you / They love you". Then: "The higher you are on the chart, the more they will pay." (Nov 26)
4. **The identity bind.** "Here's why smart people choose to ________ intead of ________". He calls it "Very casual, very under the radar" and says it "plays on the identity level, which is the level of the psyche that rules the rest." https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/1965762116755079459 (Sep 10, 2025)
5. **The self-approval test.** "Would I do this, buy this, own this, or go there if no one ever knew about it or saw it?" He adds: "All luxury, status based advertising and marketing works on the concept of self-approval". (Jul 30)
6. **Status through a humble origin story.** "I made my first million on a $95, 14-inch Dell laptop with three missing keys". https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/1950625101592014962 (Jul 30, 2025)
7. **The flex hidden in a parenthesis.** "I don't ever talk about money (even though I have more than most)". (Dec 9)

**Overt uses (don't copy):** "$80k days, $100k days, $250k days and $500k days. Yes days" (Feb 19) and "I'm able to charge $250,000 for consulting gigs" (Oct 4). By 2026 he attacks flexing himself: "Far too many "success" mfers here larping with a rented life." https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/2015546930290389437 (Jan 25, 2026). The anti-flex stance is worth borrowing, in calm language.

## 3. His best decompositions related to growth

1. **Scale vs growth, plus magnitude and the ceiling (Oct 8).**
   - Scale means no added resources. Growth means added resources.
   - Add "magnitude" ("the size of the transactions") and a 1% capture rule, and you get the "ceiling of scale".
   - Past the ceiling come "turnover pockets" and variable pricing.
2. **The money equation (Dec 15).**
   - "Scale + Magnitude = Rate And Amount of Money Acquired / Scale is the number of customers/clients. / Magnitude is the size of the transaction."
   - He maps it to three people: the offer, the media buyer and the sales rep.
   - The loop: "Get them -> Ascend them -> Repeat the process."
3. **HOW / HOW / WHAT (Sep 20).** "1. How do you make $1,000 / month (what activity)? 2. How do you repeat this 100 times (what activity)? 3. What's stopping you from repeating the activity 100 times?"
4. **Revenue bands (Jul 24).** Product and marketing, then people and teams, then systems.
5. **The level ladder (Oct 29).** VA → copywriter → C.R.O. → CMO → offer owner → publisher → owning "distribution channels". Each level "hires and pays the lower levels."
6. **Automate and delegate (Aug 18).** "Automate = removal of time / Delegate = minimization/removal of time".
7. **Capacity (Jun 13).** "Generating capacity" (more customers) vs "Increasing capacity" (serving more people at once). He says to zig-zag between them, "repeat forever".
8. **The million broken down (Feb 2, 2025).** https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/1886122359767121951
   - "$1,000,000/12 = $83,333.33 / month" → per week → "about $3k per day" → "30 sales per day" → "600 people".
   - His reason for doing it: "if you've never made a million dollars, your mind will not KNOW how to make a million dollars. It does not have a map".
9. **Two of three.**
   - In 2024 the three traits were smart, hardworking and good timing: "having two out of the three is all you need" (Aug 14).
   - In 2026 they became money, work ethic and skills: "They used two to get the third." https://x.com/IAmAlenSultanic/status/2021954088628347154
10. **The loss audit (Nov 15, Apr 19).**
   - Name the loss, then ask "What do I gain out of losing this?", then find a way to "have both".
   - His example: "making $10 million and you don't have time? Well, peel off $1m and buy back your time". (Apr 19)
11. **Inclusion vs exclusion (Dec 20).** "How many want to give you money vs how many actually can give you money? [...] The rate limiter of your business is the size of your audience, and their rate limiter is their income."
12. **Who isn't buying (Nov 6).** The Amazon category sequence, used as a question you can ask again and again.

## 4. Thirty growth and status moves for $1M–$20M owners [OURS]

Every example below is ours, in Josh's calm Australian voice. Keep any numbers illustrative, and add "Not financial advice." wherever structure comes up.

1. **Name the ceiling.** "Every owner I talk to hits the same wall. Not because they're bad at it. The level they're playing at has a roof." [OURS]
2. **Separate scale from growth.** "Hiring another tech is growth. You've grown the revenue and the headaches. Buying a business that already has the techs is a different thing." [OURS]
3. **Transcend the level instead of polishing it.** "You can keep getting better at running one shop, or become the bloke who owns three. Different jobs." [OURS]
4. **Use the revenue bands.** "Under five mil it's product and marketing. Five to ten, it's people. Past ten, it's systems. A good acquisition can skip a few years in the middle." [OURS]
5. **Frequency, not a new activity.** "You already know how to run one profitable business. The question is how you do it again, not how you learn something new." [OURS]
6. **Ask what's stopping repetition.** "What's stopping you doing that twice? Usually it's not money. It's not knowing how deals get structured." [OURS]
7. **Non-linear jumps.** "Organic growth is a slope. Buying a business is a step." [OURS]
8. **The market's ceiling.** "If your area only has so many customers, more ads just make each one dearer. Buying the competitor brings the customers who were never going to switch." [OURS]
9. **Who isn't buying from you?** "Who isn't giving you money right now? Often it's your competitor's customers, and they come with the business." [OURS]
10. **Shorten the timeline.** "You could build a second location over five years, or buy one that's already built. Same destination, shorter road." [OURS]
11. **Grow the top, don't only cut.** "Cost-cutting has a floor. A second set of customers doesn't." [OURS]
12. **Own the whole pie.** "I'd rather own all of a small business than ten per cent of someone else's story." [OURS]
13. **The supplier or neighbour precedent.** "Some famous brands were bought by the people who supplied them. The bloke down the road is a natural buyer." [OURS]
14. **Money in the bank, not top-line revenue.** "Don't ask what a business turns over. Ask what's left after the debt, and whether it covers itself about one and a half times." [OURS]
15. **Renewable revenue.** "When you buy a business you're buying years of customers who already pay. That's revenue over time, not a sale." [OURS]
16. **Product over talent.** "If it only works because the old owner is there, you're buying a job. Look for one that runs on process." [OURS]
17. **Time is the first limit.** "If you're the bottleneck in one business, two makes it worse. Free your time first, then buy." [OURS]
18. **Be the one with leverage.** "If you're still on the tools, you're the leverage. Owning the second business makes you the one who has it." [OURS]
19. **Decisions are the bottleneck.** "Most owners decide in two minutes and take two years to give themselves permission. Good deals don't wait that long." [OURS]
20. **Run a loss audit.** "What do you think you'd lose by buying a second business? Write it down, then work out how to keep it and still do the deal." [OURS]
21. **Who you become.** "The question isn't whether you can afford it. It's who you'd be if you owned three businesses instead of one." [OURS]
22. **The definition trap.** "'A plumber who owns a business' has a ceiling. 'An owner of plumbing businesses' is a different job." [OURS]
23. **Change the room.** "If everyone around you thinks buying a business needs millions in the bank, you'll think it too. Spend time with people who've done a deal." [OURS]
24. **Look at them, not up at them.** "Owners who've bought three businesses aren't smarter than you. They just learnt how vendor finance works earlier." [OURS]
25. **Rich vs wealthy.** "Rich is money with no time. Wealthy is both. A business that runs without you is the second one." [OURS]
26. **Status lowers the seller's risk.** "A retiring owner doesn't always sell to the highest bidder. They sell to someone they trust with their staff and their name." [OURS]
27. **The trophy chain.** "The first deal is the hard one. After that, people start bringing deals to you." [OURS]
28. **A soft identity bind.** "Here's why smart owners buy their competitor instead of trying to out-advertise them." [OURS]
29. **Legacy as stewardship.** "The owner down the road spent thirty years building something. Buying it well is how it stays in the town." [OURS]
30. **Look back before looking up.** "Look at what you've already built. Doing it again is a smaller step than doing it the first time." [OURS]

## 5. What not to copy

- **Profanity.**
  - "There's levels to this sh**", "big dick with numbers", "fu**ed over", "Fuck Lambos", "broke AF", "bat-shit insane".
- **The mystical register.**
  - "the universe opens it's doors", "Magick", "Avra k'davra", the acorn post (Nov 4), "To manifest" (Jun 21, 2026), the "birthright" frame (Mar 8, 2026).
  - Keep the logic and drop the metaphysics.
- **Income flexing.**
  - "$500k days", "$250,000 consulting gigs", "I can beat just about any copywriter in the game" (Oct 29), villas and yachts, "I have more than most".
  - "Money over being liked", as in "did you come to play the game to be liked or to get rich?" (Aug 17). It clashes with the trust a retiring seller needs.
- **His brand phrases.**
  - "Food for thought, you do the dishes", "the game" as a constant noun, "the almighty algo", "flip over", "internet marketing math", "biz-op", "Mt. Olympus of marketing", "All for love of the game".
  - NHB, NHB+ and FF references.
- **His loose statistics.**
  - "capture 1% of the market", "10% of their annual revenue", "3 sales reps [...] $300k a month", and the brand-ownership claims in the Dec 20 post, some of which are wrong.
  - Don't repeat any of these as fact.
- **Status hacks.**
  - Fake "best seller" listings and celebrity-name press releases (Oct 4). They're off-brand and misleading.
- **Promises we can't make.**
  - "you'll get your turn, you'll get your wins, and you'll get your millions" (Oct 26).
  - "now you can too" (Sep 15, 2025).
  - The series makes no income promises.
