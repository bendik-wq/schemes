## EP 57 · Every level from "in the owner's head" to sellable: 10 SOPs in 1 week (step-by-step)

There's a well-kept secret about small businesses. Most of them run on one person's memory.

One number: labour cost per revenue dollar. As the business grows, it should fall. Not because anyone's pay is cut. Because new hires get productive faster and fewer jobs get redone.
