# How to use this

Every title here uses the structure of a real Daniel Iles or Jeremy Haynes title. Only the topic changes, to an owner's pain or the money in a deal.

Three rules:

1. **Lead with the pain or the number.** An owner clicks for "my business can't run without me" or "$800K cash vs $1.3M over time", not for "deal structuring".
2. **Only use numbers we can back.** They come from Josh's record or from the worked example in the script. Never put an income claim in a title.
3. **The title must match the script.** If the video doesn't answer the title in the first 30 seconds, change the title.
